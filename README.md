**Explore. Discover. Play. Learn. Remember.**

MinneTrail is a free, family-friendly Android adventure game designed to encourage children aged 4–8 and their parents to spend more time outdoors, discover new places, complete exciting missions and create unforgettable memories together.

By combining real-world exploration, educational activities, photography and creative challenges, MinneTrail transforms everyday family outings into exciting adventures.

## 🎮 Game Features

### 🗺️ Outdoor Adventures & Missions

- Discover exciting outdoor missions and challenges.
- Explore nature, parks, forests and urban environments.
- Complete educational and creative activities.
- Learn about the world through real-life experiences.
- Take photographs while completing missions.
- Track completed missions and adventure progress.
- Enjoy activities designed for children and parents to complete together.

### 🏆 XP & Rewards

- Earn experience points (XP) by completing missions.
- Collect rewards as you progress through the game.
- Celebrate achievements and completed adventures.
- Track individual progress.
- Earn additional XP by discovering city landmarks.
- Duplicate XP protection prevents collecting the same reward repeatedly.

### 👧 Child Profiles

- Create individual child profiles.
- Keep track of each child's adventures and progress.
- Build a personalised collection of childhood memories.
- Reset game progress when starting a new adventure.

### 🌍 Discover Your City

Explore cities, famous landmarks and historical locations through child-friendly descriptions, interesting facts and interactive challenges.

**Currently featured destinations:**

🇸🇪 **Sweden**
- Gothenburg
- Stockholm

Each featured location includes:

- Child-friendly descriptions.
- Interesting and educational facts.
- Mini challenges.
- Location-specific missions.
- Visit tracking.
- 50 XP for each newly completed location.
- Photography challenges.
- Google Maps integration for navigation.

The city exploration system is designed to support additional countries, cities and destinations in the future.

### 📸 Photography & Memory Album

Turn every adventure into a lasting memory.

- Take photographs directly within the application.
- Import existing photographs from your device.
- Capture memories while completing outdoor missions.
- Take photographs during city exploration challenges.
- Automatically collect adventure photographs in a shared memory album.
- Browse your collection of family adventures.
- Share your favourite memories.
- Print photographs and creative projects.

### 🎨 Personalised Coloring Pages

Transform your own adventure photographs into creative coloring pages.

- Generate coloring pages from personal photographs.
- Convert photographs into black-and-white illustrations.
- Adjust the illustration detail level.
- Create personalised coloring activities.
- Print coloring pages for offline creative play.
- Combine outdoor exploration with artistic creativity.

### 💌 Personalised Postcards

- Create postcards using photographs from your adventures.
- Turn special moments into personalised keepsakes.
- Share postcards with family and friends.
- Print your favourite creations.

### 📖 Family Memory Book

Create a unique collection of your family's adventures.

- Turn collected photographs into a personalised memory book.
- Generate PDF albums.
- Preserve special moments and completed adventures.
- Share digital memory books with family and friends.
- Print your family adventure album.
- Create lasting keepsakes of childhood experiences.

## 🛡️ Child Safety & Privacy

MinneTrail is designed with children and families in mind.

- Family-friendly content.
- Age-appropriate missions and challenges.
- No mandatory subscriptions.
- Parental PIN protection for sensitive actions in the Google Play version.
- Parental controls for sharing, printing, external links and resetting progress.
- No location permission required in the Google Play version.
- Encourages real-world activities instead of excessive screen time.

## 💚 Our Mission

MinneTrail was created with a simple philosophy: childhood should be filled with exploration, imagination, creativity and meaningful experiences.

Rather than encouraging children to spend more time looking at screens, MinneTrail uses technology as a tool to inspire real-world adventures.

Every mission is an opportunity to discover something new. Every photograph is a memory. Every adventure is a moment worth keeping.
